Changed: a cache read that finds the entry briefly locked by a concurrent write now retries once, after about 1 ms, instead of counting as a miss, so the request is served from the cache rather than unoptimized. If the retry finds it locked again the read is a miss as before. Two new counters on `/v1/metrics` show how often this happens: `pagespeed_cache_busy_reads_total` (reads retried) and `pagespeed_cache_busy_retry_hits_total` (retries that found the entry).
