- **Negative rewrite deadlines now mean "wait up to a bound, then serve the original".** If you set `RewriteDeadlinePerFlushMs` or `InPlaceRewriteDeadlineMs` below zero to wait for optimization, requests for rewritten resources (`.pagespeed.` URLs and in-place resources) now wait up to `NegativeDeadlineFetchWaitMs` (new; 1000 ms by default, at most `FetcherTimeOutMs`, server or virtual-host configuration only) and then serve the original, with `Cache-Control: private` and a lifetime of at most 5 minutes, instead of waiting indefinitely. A combined script request waits up to `FetcherTimeOutMs` (5 seconds by default), then answers 503 with `Retry-After` and `Cache-Control: no-store`. To wait longer, raise `NegativeDeadlineFetchWaitMs` (and `FetcherTimeOutMs`). Rewriting of HTML pages still waits as before.
