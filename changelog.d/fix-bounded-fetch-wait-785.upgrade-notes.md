- **Negative rewrite deadlines now mean "wait up to a bound, then serve the original".** If you set `RewriteDeadlinePerFlushMs` or `InPlaceRewriteDeadlineMs` below zero to wait for optimization, requests for rewritten resources (`.pagespeed.` URLs and in-place resources) now wait up to the default rewrite deadline (10 ms) and then serve the original, with `Cache-Control: private` and a lifetime of at most 5 minutes, instead of waiting indefinitely. A combined script request waits up to the blocking fetch timeout (5 seconds by default), then answers 503 with `Retry-After`. To wait longer for optimized resources, use a positive deadline instead of a negative one. Rewriting of HTML pages still waits as before.
